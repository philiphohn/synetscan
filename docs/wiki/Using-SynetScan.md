# Using SynetScan

How to read SynetScan's pages and get your daily work done in them.

- **[Interface Tour](Interface-Tour)**: the four pages (Overview, Devices, Activity, Settings), the status area, menus and keyboard shortcuts.
- **[Understanding Devices and Their Status](Devices-and-Status)**: Online and Offline, New and Unconfirmed, device types, badges, and how SynetScan chooses a device's name.
- **[Reviewing Newly Discovered Devices](Reviewing-New-Devices)**: find out what a new device is and mark it as known.
- **[Managing Names, Types, and Notes](Names-Types-and-Notes)**: give devices your own names, correct their type, write notes.
- **[Activity Events and Scan History](Activity-and-Scan-History)**: what the activity log records and how to search it.
- **[Device Actions and CSV Export](Device-Actions-and-CSV-Export)**: Open Web Interface, Wake-on-LAN, copy addresses, delete devices, export the list.
